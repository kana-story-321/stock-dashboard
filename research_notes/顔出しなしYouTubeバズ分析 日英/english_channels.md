# Viral / Fast-Growing Faceless YouTube Channels in English-Speaking Markets (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-09-29. ~17 tool calls. Many per-channel stats come from secondary aggregator blogs (faceless.my, OutlierKit, Fliki, etc.) that are frequently SEO/AI-tool marketing sites; treat their numbers as approximate. Hard news sources (Guardian, 404 Media, Deadline, Fortune, Tubefilter, Kapwing) are prioritized for breakout/crackdown cases.

## Which faceless genres grew fastest in 2025–2026 and which are declining?

### Takeaway
The fastest growth in 2025 came from fully AI-generated Shorts/animation ("AI slop": anthropomorphic animal melodramas, fake movie trailers, AI "history for sleep" long-form, AI-narrated stories), fueled by Veo 3 / Grok Imagine. From July 2025 onward, and sharply from Dec 2025–Jan 2026, YouTube's "inauthentic content" enforcement terminated or demonetized the biggest mass-produced AI channels, so template AI slop, fake trailers, and untransformed Reddit-story/TTS formats are the declining/at-risk segment in 2026, while research-driven documentary/explainer, finance/AI-tutorial, and established animation brands remain durable.

### Cited Findings
**Growth of AI-only channels (2025)**
- Guardian analysis of Playboard data: of the 100 fastest-growing YouTube channels globally in July 2025, 9 showed purely AI-generated content; all 9 had >1M subscribers, largest ~5M; five were new, three gained nearly all their subscribers in July (up to +98.6% in the month). After the Guardian contacted YouTube, 3 channels were removed and 2 blocked from ad revenue — [Macao News reprint of Guardian report](https://macaonews.org/news/around-the-world/ai-slop-youtube/); [Guardian via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/stories/newspaper/The-Guardian/AICREATED-VIDEOS-ARE-FLOODING-YOUTUBE-EXPERTS-SAY)
- Examples in the Guardian piece: a "baby crawling into a pre-launch space rocket" channel (1.6M subs) and Super Cat League (3.9M subs, human-like cats having affairs etc.). Surge attributed to Google Veo 3 and Grok Imagine — [search summary of Guardian coverage](https://macaonews.org/news/around-the-world/ai-slop-youtube/)
- Kapwing (Nov 2025; data collected to Oct 2025, top 100 trending channels per country from Playboard, metrics from Social Blade): 278 of ~15,000 top channels are AI-slop-only, together >63B views, 221M subscribers, ~$117M/yr estimated revenue. US has 9 trending AI slop channels in its top 100: combined 14.47M subs, 3.39B views. 21% (104/500) of first Shorts shown to a new account were AI slop; ~33% were "brainrot" — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)
- AI "Boring History / history for sleep" long-form boomed in 2025: channels publish hours of AI videos daily; e.g., "Sleepless Historian" exceeded ~600K subscribers; dozens of "sleepy historian" clones — [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/shi-vyhaduie-istoriiu-na-youtube-zhenerovani-video-vytisniaiut-spravzhni-znannia-1757324766); [Slashdot (Sept 2025)](https://news.slashdot.org/story/25/09/03/2028206/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-drowning-out-real-history)
- Aggregator claims "AI news and tutorial" channels are the fastest-growing faceless category in 2026 and AI storytelling (sci-fi, mystery, "what if") is a fast-growing format — [Fliki](https://fliki.ai/blog/faceless-youtube-channel-ideas) (marketing source, low reliability)

**Decline / enforcement (mid-2025 → 2026)**
- July 15, 2025: YouTube renamed "repetitious content" monetization rule to "inauthentic content", covering mass-produced/repetitive content — [Medium analysis](https://mateostarcevicfilipovic.medium.com/youtube-quietly-demonetizes-faceless-ai-channels-since-july-2025-30b3d2daaf2e); [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Dec 2025: YouTube terminated fake AI movie trailer channels Screen Culture (India) and KH Studio (Georgia), together >2M subs and >1B views, after they were demonetized earlier in 2025 following a Deadline investigation, got reinstated by adding "fan trailer/concept trailer" labels, then relapsed into spam/misleading-metadata violations — [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2025/12/youtube-terminates-screen-culture-kh-studio-fake-ai-trailer-1236652506/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/tech/youtube-terminates-screen-culture-kh-studio-over-ai-fake-movie-trailers/)
- Jan 2026: YouTube removed 16 AI-slop channels totaling ~4.7B views, ~35M subs, ~$10M/yr estimated revenue; named include CuentosFacianantes (5.95M subs, Dragon Ball-themed AI, ~$2.66M est. annual earnings), Imperio de Jesus, Super Cat League (4.21M), Héroes de Fantasía, Bandar Apna Dost; YouTube cited spam and deceptive-practices policies, not a new anti-AI rule — [XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/youtube-just-deleted-over-4-7-billion-views-worth-ofai-slop-videos/); [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/01/youtube-wiped-ai-slop-erasing-4-7-billion-views-from-low-quality-content.html); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/). Note: most named channels are Spanish/Hindi/non-English; CuentosFacianantes is US-based but Spanish-language (Kapwing).
- OutlierKit also reports "Three Minute Wisdom" (1.7M subs, ~2B views) had most content removed — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/) (single secondary source)
- July 13, 2026: YouTube clarified (no new rules) three demonetization buckets: (1) generic/repetitive templated content incl. "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates"; (2) "unsatisfying or off-putting" emotionally manipulative content, e.g., "repetitive scenarios of animals in exaggerated distress"; (3) AI personas posing as experts on health/legal/financial/political topics (AI "doctors", AI "podcast hosts" giving financial tips) — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)
- March 2026: YouTube tested viewer pop-ups rating "AI slop" (5-point scale); automatic AI labeling using detection signals/SynthID. Collateral damage reported: human faceless creators say they're being demonetized — Doctor NOS (1.7M subs): "most of them are getting demonetised"; some faceless creators now hire on-camera hosts via Fiverr/Upwork — [The Next Web, June 15 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)
- Feb 2025: "True Crime Case Files" — 150+ fabricated AI murder stories presented as real; one fake Colorado case ~2M views; scripts half ChatGPT; channel terminated — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/a-true-crime-documentary-series-has-millions-of-views-the-murders-are-all-ai-generated/); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/02/14/youtube-true-crime-case-files-ai-crime-channel-terminated/)

### Inferences
- Growth ranking 2025: AI animal/character melodrama Shorts ≈ AI kids/brainrot > AI fake trailers > AI "history for sleep" long-form > AI storytelling/Reddit-TTS. By 2026 the first three are the most enforcement-exposed (fake trailers & "animals in distress" are named explicitly); sleep-history is still profitable but fits the "generic template" bucket.
- Durable faceless genres in 2026 are those with visible human craft: researched documentaries (business/tech/history), hand-made 2D/3D animation brands, curated compilations with commentary, finance/AI tutorials with original scripts. Lofi/ambient remains stable but low-RPM and crowded.
- "Faceless" itself isn't being demonetized; mass production without transformation is. Human faceless creators report algorithmic headwinds, suggesting a partial swing toward on-camera presence.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2026 Playboard/Social Blade "fastest growing" list specifically for English faceless channels was retrieved.
- No hard data on Reddit-story Shorts channels' 2025–26 decline (only generic claims); treat "decline" as inference from policy.
- Could not confirm which of the 9 US AI-slop top-100 channels in Kapwing's report are English-language.

## Specific channel examples with subscribers, views, cadence, Shorts vs long-form

### Takeaway
The largest English faceless brands are long-established (Bright Side, 5-Minute Crafts, Kurzgesagt, Lofi Girl, Cocomelon, DaFuq!?Boom!); 2025–26 breakouts were mostly AI-generated channels in the 1–6M range, several of which were subsequently terminated. Channel-level cadence/view data for 2026 is thin in reliable sources.

- ColdFusion ~5.2M and Wendover Productions ~4.9M (documentary niche leaders); MagnatesMedia cited as model for business docs (voiceover + stock footage) — [faceless.my](https://faceless.my/youtube/top-faceless-youtube-channels/) (2026 aggregator; MagnatesMedia sub count not found)

He expects the window to profit to close "around 2027" — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/12/30/ai-slop-faceless-youtube-accounts-adavia-davis-user-generated-content/)

**AI animation / AI slop Shorts (breakouts)**
- Super Cat League: 3.9M (July 2025) → 4.21M, then terminated Jan 2026 — [Guardian via Macao News](https://macaonews.org/news/around-the-world/ai-slop-youtube/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/)
- "Space baby" AI channel: 1.6M (July 2025) — [Guardian summary](https://www.magzter.com/stories/newspaper/The-Guardian/AICREATED-VIDEOS-ARE-FLOODING-YOUTUBE-EXPERTS-SAY)
- Screen Culture + KH Studio (English fake trailers): >2M subs combined, >1B views, terminated Dec 2025 — [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2025/12/youtube-terminates-screen-culture-kh-studio-fake-ai-trailer-1236652506/)

**True crime narration**
- True Crime Case Files (AI-fabricated): 150+ videos, top ~2M views, terminated Feb 2025 — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/a-true-crime-documentary-series-has-millions-of-views-the-murders-are-all-ai-generated/)

**Reddit stories Shorts**
- Format: TTS narration over Minecraft parkour / Subway Surfers; claimed 65–80% watch-through; channels claimed to reach 100K–1M+ subs in a year, individual videos 5M+ views — [GhostShorts blog](https://ghostshorts.com/blog/how-to-make-reddit-story-videos-for-tiktok-and-youtube-shorts-2026) (tool vendor; unverified). Named as a textbook inauthentic-content example ("same voice, same music, same visuals, different Reddit story") — [search summary of YT policy analyses](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ai-policy)

### Inferences
- Success factors across durable channels: consistent narrator voice, recognizable visual identity, narrow niche, high average view duration, script depth, and diversified revenue (sponsors, Patreon, products) — per [faceless.my](https://faceless.my/youtube/top-faceless-youtube-channels/). AI breakouts instead relied on volume (multiple uploads/day), Shorts virality, emotional/shock hooks, and cheap per-video cost.
- Long-form sleep/history content monetizes far better per view-hour than Shorts slop, explaining the shift of AI operators toward 3–6 hour videos.

- Upload cadence and typical views per video mostly unavailable from primary sources; Social Blade/Playboard pages were not fetched directly.

## Notable breakout cases in 2025–2026 (explode then demonetized/terminated)

### Takeaway
The canonical English-relevant arc: rapid AI-fueled growth (Super Cat League, space-baby, Screen Culture/KH Studio, True Crime Case Files, AI sleep-history) → media exposure (Guardian, Deadline, 404 Media, Kapwing) → demonetization → termination (Feb 2025, Dec 2025, Jan 2026 waves) → July 2026 policy clarification.

### Cited Findings
- Feb 2025 True Crime Case Files terminated after 404 Media exposé — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/02/14/youtube-true-crime-case-files-ai-crime-channel-terminated/)
- Early/mid 2025 Deadline investigation → Screen Culture & KH Studio demonetized → reinstated with "fan/concept trailer" labels → terminated Dec 2025 — [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2025/12/youtube-terminates-screen-culture-kh-studio-fake-ai-trailer-1236652506/)
- Aug 2025 Guardian contact → 3 AI channels removed, 2 demonetized — [Macao News/Guardian](https://macaonews.org/news/around-the-world/ai-slop-youtube/)
- Jan 2026 16-channel purge (4.7B views, 35M subs, ~$10M/yr) — [XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/youtube-just-deleted-over-4-7-billion-views-worth-ofai-slop-videos/)
- Jul 2026 policy clarification naming animal-distress and AI-expert persona content — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)
- Counter-example still profiting (as of Dec 2025): Adavia Davis's AI Boring History network ~$700K/yr — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/12/30/ai-slop-faceless-youtube-accounts-adavia-davis-user-generated-content/)

### Inferences
- Media exposure appears to be a key trigger for enforcement; channels built on deception (fake trailers, fake crimes) get terminated, while "merely low-effort" channels more often get demonetized.

### Gaps
- Current (Sept 2026) status of Adavia Davis's channels and Sleepless Historian unknown.

## Typical RPM ranges per genre (English markets)

### Takeaway
Finance/investing is highest ($8–22+ RPM), tech/education mid ($5–10), history/true crime ~$5–12, gaming/entertainment low ($1–3), sleep/ASMR/lofi/scary stories lowest ($0.5–5), and Shorts are a tiny fraction (CPM cited as low as $0.02). All RPM figures come from creator-tool blogs, not YouTube; treat as indicative.

### Cited Findings
- OutlierKit (data from Virlo, checked Sept 28 2026): Investing $11–22; Personal finance $8.25–13.75; Real estate $8.25–16.50; Make money online $6.60–11; Digital marketing $5.50–9.90; Tech reviews $5.50–9.90; Education $4.95–8.25; Fitness $3.30–6.60; Gaming $1.65–3.30; Entertainment $1.10–3.30 — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/most-profitable-youtube-niches)
- Shorts: creators receive 45% of their allocated Shorts pool; CPMs "often as little as $0.02" — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/most-profitable-youtube-niches)
- Finance, B2B software, AI tutorials: $14–38 faceless RPM; personal finance $15–25 US; luxury $12–18; health $10–18; self-improvement/data comparisons/history $5–11; true crime $5–12; sleep $2–5; relaxing/sleep music, ASMR, scary stories $0.50–2.50 — [aggregated search summary of Fliki/kineclip/youtubeniches](https://fliki.ai/blog/best-faceless-youtube-niches); [kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/best-niches-faceless-youtube-2026/)
- General knowledge channels $3–8, finance/business $15–40 (CPM) — [faceless.my](https://faceless.my/youtube/top-faceless-youtube-channels/)
- Kapwing estimated Bandar Apna Dost (India, 2.4B views) could earn up to $4.25M/yr — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)

### Inferences
- Sources conflict on finance RPM ($8–14 per OutlierKit/Virlo vs $15–25 per other blogs) — likely CPM vs RPM confusion; a conservative US faceless finance RPM is ~$8–20.
- Low-RPM genres (sleep, lofi, AI history) compensate with extreme watch time (6-hour videos, 24/7 streams), which is why they're attractive to AI operators despite low RPM.

### Gaps
- No primary/YouTube-disclosed RPM data; no UK/CA/AU-specific RPM breakdown found; kids (Made for Kids) RPM not found.
