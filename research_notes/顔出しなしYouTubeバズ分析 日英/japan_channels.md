# Faceless ("顔出しなし" / 非属人化) YouTube Channels in the Japanese Market, 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-09-29. Tracker sites (ユーチュラ tag pages: HTTP 403; youtubernext: 503) could not be fetched directly. Social Blade, NoxInfluencer and kamui tracker were not reached within the tool budget. Subscriber figures below come from the sources cited, each labeled with its date. Revenue figures from tuber-ch.com and M&A listings are third-party estimates or seller claims, not verified.

## Q1. Which faceless genres are growing fastest in Japan in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Growth in 2025–2026 is concentrated in four areas: (1) character-voice "explainer" channels (ゆっくり / ずんだもん / VOICEVOX), especially history, current events, geopolitics and science; (2) 2ch/スカッと story channels, particularly on Shorts; (3) studio-produced original short anime such as Plott's channels; (4) AI-assisted Shorts (AI animals/babies, "日本称賛" content, space mysteries), plus stock-type BGM/sleep content. Offsetting this, the 2025-07-15 YPP "inauthentic content" update was followed by a wave of monetization suspensions in 2026 Q1. The wave hit static-image + synthetic-voice template channels hardest.

### Cited Findings
- A Jan 2026 note article says AI has made quality content easy to produce, and that successful 非属人化 (faceless) channels are rapidly increasing in Japan and abroad. — [AIJ note, 2026-01-08](https://note.com/dapper_poppy7392/n/nebe1d40bc7fa)
- ASMR and 作業用BGM are described as "stock-type content" that keeps earning views long after upload, crosses language barriers and attracts overseas viewers. — [Filmora JP 2026](https://filmora.wondershare.jp/youtube-video-editing/without-showing-face.html) / [search summary](https://rakkoma.com/media/youtube-15-genres-make-money/)
- Faceless genres listed as viable for 2026: game commentary, anime/manga explanation, narration/audiobooks (朗読), clip compilations (切り抜き), ASMR/ambient, and product reviews voiced with ずんだもん. — [ラッコ大福帳, 2025-09-30](https://rakkoma.com/media/youtube-15-genres-make-money/)
- ずんだもん explainer channels on history, current events and international affairs are described as currently growing fast, run with no face and no human voice. One operator claims 20,000+ subscribers in 33 days with ずんだもん (2025). — [Brain media](https://media.brain-market.com/zundamon-youtube/)
- An M&A listing shows a history/society ずんだもん channel with 19,000+ subscribers that monetized in 2 months. A science ずんだもん channel (6,000+ subs) reportedly monetized on its first video. — [ラッコM&A #9554](https://rakkoma.com/project/detail/9554); [サイトストック #9538](https://sitestock.jp/sales_web_detail.html?id=9538&from_page=sales_web_list)
- Vlog × ずんだもん is a 2025 hybrid format that monetized within 1 month. — [リベシティ](https://library.libecity.com/articles/01K829G932DJPDHMS5BRRWASZP)
- 猫ミーム started trending around Feb 2024 and became a major Shorts genre in H1 2024. It is said to have cooled in Japan but had a revival after W&W's spinning-cat EDM track (Jan 2025, 200M views in 4 months). — [find-model / search summary](https://find-model.jp/insta-lab/sns-trend-cat-meme/); [ラッコM&A #15999 "再ブーム到来中の猫ミーム"](https://rakkoma.com/project/detail/15999)
- AI animal/baby videos (AI猫動画, 赤ちゃん×動物) are described as a trending viral Shorts format in 2025–2026. — [SHIFT AI](https://shift-ai.co.jp/blog/39531/); [noviai](https://www.noviai.ai/jp/video-tips/ai-cat-video/)
- Japan's vertical short-video ad market grew 171.1% YoY in 2024 to ¥90B (per Itera). Its stated 2026 SNS ad total of ¥1.1008T is a projection. — [Itera](https://itera.co.jp/blog/short-trend-2026)
- **Headwind:** On 2025-07-15, YouTube updated YPP guidelines on "mass-produced, repetitive content." Google Japan told ITmedia the change "does not target AI content" as such, only repetitive, low-quality, inauthentic content. — [ITmedia AI+, 2025-07-10](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/aiplus/articles/2507/10/news128.html); [Gizmodo JP](https://www.gizmodo.jp/2025/07/youtube-cant-put-pandoras-ai-slop-back-in-the-box.html)
- Examples of content at risk under the update: AI-voice narration with no personal commentary, slideshow compilations of reused clips, and summary/reaction videos with little original insight. — [LAUGHS inc.](https://laughsinc.jp/youtube202507/) / [KASOU](https://kasoudesign.com/knowledge/youtube-ai/)
- From January 2026, monetization of AI-centric channels was stopped en masse in Japan, according to operator reports. — [KASOU 2026](https://kasoudesign.com/knowledge/youtube-ai/); [note ボヤキング](https://note.com/id_x/n/necd893b3e369)
- A press release from 日本動画センター (2026-04-15) analyzed 50+ channels suspended in 2026 Q1:
  - Genre at risk: ゆっくり解説 built from still art + fixed background + VOICEVOX narration.
  - Claimed triggers: the same artwork reused 3+ times across 20 videos, more than 47% thumbnail-template duplication, and 60%+ script similarity.
  - One channel was restored in 17 days after adding personal anecdotes, manually edited audio and original visuals.
  - Caveat: this is a vendor PR; the thresholds are its own claims and YouTube has not confirmed them. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000030.000166228.html) / [西日本新聞 mirror](https://www.nishinippon.co.jp/item/1482007/)
- In Feb 2026, investment creator かぶまる reportedly deleted 6 years of videos after losing monetization. The same article says some ゆっくり/ずんだもん channels were being reinstated after differentiating their content. — [note SS@ゆっくり編集効率化, 2026-02-20](https://note.com/yukkuri_auto/n/nc6b582539c26)

### Inferences
- The fastest *new-channel* growth appears to be in ずんだもん/VOICEVOX explainers (history, geopolitics, science) and AI-assisted Shorts. However, 2026 Q1 turned "template 量産" (template mass-production) into a monetization risk. The durable winners are likely channels with original scripts, animation or motion, and distinct characters.
- Studio-grade original animation (Plott) and stock-type BGM/sleep content look structurally less exposed to the inauthentic-content crackdown than static-image voice channels.

### Gaps
- No quantitative genre-level growth ranking (e.g., kamui tracker genre growth) could be retrieved.
- No confirmed data on how many large Japanese channels were demonetized, beyond vendor and blog claims.

## Q2. Example channels with subscribers, views, cadence and format

### Takeaway
The largest verified faceless successes are studio-made short anime (Plott: 4 channels at 1M+) and older ゆっくり explainer channels (200K–500K subs as of 2022). The 2ch/スカッと Shorts genre has at least one channel with 1.3B total views. Many fast-growing 2025–2026 examples are small (1K–30K) channels sold on M&A marketplaces.

## Q3. Success factors cited

### Takeaway
The sources repeatedly cite the same factors:
- A recognisable character voice (ゆっくり/ずんだもん/VOICEVOX) paired with strong topic selection.
- Original scripts and visual movement, which since 2026 are needed to stay monetized.
- Daily Shorts for discovery, combined with long-form for RPM.
- Serialized characters and IP collaborations for animation.
- Watch-time-heavy long videos for sleep and BGM content.

### Cited Findings
- ゆっくり channels differ by topic and dialogue quality rather than format. — [yoshirushi](https://yoshirushi.com/youtube-yukkuri/)
- Plott's growth drivers: daily Shorts, a 10-minute long-form series, backstory "story editions," celebrity and IP collaborations, and character cover songs. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000133.000047837.html)
- Low-cost AI production pipelines (ChatGPT scripts, AI images, Vrew editing) combined with outsourcing and daily posting are cited as enabling monetization within months. — [AIJ note](https://note.com/dapper_poppy7392/n/nebe1d40bc7fa)
- The 2026 YouTube algorithm is said to favour channels posting both Shorts and long-form. This is a blog claim. — [search summary: Itera/others](https://itera.co.jp/blog/short-trend-2026)
- Steps for monetization recovery: personal commentary, manual audio edits, original visuals, and avoiding reused 立ち絵 (character art) and templated thumbnails. — [PR TIMES 日本動画センター](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000030.000166228.html); [note](https://note.com/yukkuri_auto/n/nc6b582539c26)
- "Popular genre × your own attribute" is recommended to find blue-ocean niches, e.g. 40代向けプチプラ (budget buys for people in their 40s). — [ラッコ大福帳](https://rakkoma.com/media/youtube-15-genres-make-money/)
- Monetization thresholds: 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. — [ラッコ大福帳](https://rakkoma.com/media/youtube-15-genres-make-money/)

### Inferences
- The field has shifted from "template + synthetic voice at volume" (pre-2025) to "character IP + original scripting + motion" (2026).

### Gaps
- No quantitative thumbnail/title A/B data specific to Japanese faceless channels was found.

## Q4. RPM/CPM ranges in Japan

### Takeaway
Most faceless entertainment genres earn about ¥80–400 RPM on long-form. Education/explainer content earns about ¥200–600, and finance ¥800–2,500. Shorts earn roughly 1/10–1/5 of long-form RPM, which explains how high-view Shorts channels can still earn little.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 Japan RPM by genre (the source also gives CPM):

| Genre | RPM (¥) | CPM (¥) |
|---|---|---|
| Finance | 800–2,500 | 2,000–6,000 |
| Real estate | 600–1,800 | — |
| Gadgets/tech | 300–800 | — |
| Education/language | 200–600 | — |
| Vlog | 150–450 | — |
| Entertainment/comedy | 100–400 | — |
| Gaming | 100–350 | — |
| Anime/2D | 80–280 | 200–700 |
| Kids | 50–200 | — |

  - Shorts earn 1/10–1/5 of long-form RPM.
  - The source's formula: RPM ≈ CPM × 0.55 × ad fill rate; Japanese RPM is typically 20–40% of CPM.
  - [aiseo-llmo, updated 2026-09-25](https://aiseo-llmo.com/articles/youtube-cpm-rpm-by-genre-japan-2026)
- Alternative 2025 benchmark: videos under 8 minutes earn ¥0.05–0.3 per view (RPM ¥50–300); 8 minutes and over earn ¥0.3–0.7 (RPM ¥300–700); high-value genres earn ¥0.5–1.0+. — [UREBAラボ / デジハク search summary](https://ureba.jp/lab/current-youtube-ad-cpm/)
- Illustrative Shorts economics from two AI channels:
  - The space-mystery channel earns about ¥2,459/month from about 920K lifetime views.
  - The 日本称賛 channel (20K subs, daily posts) earns about ¥63K/month.
  - [AIJ note](https://note.com/dapper_poppy7392/n/nebe1d40bc7fa)
- Sleep/narration long-form is described as "high RPM despite low views," a qualitative claim only. — [AIJ note](https://note.com/dapper_poppy7392/n/nebe1d40bc7fa)

### Inferences
- Implied RPM for スカッと屋さん: ¥1.36M from about 1.31B lifetime views is not directly comparable, but it suggests Shorts-scale economics of roughly ¥10–60 per 1,000 views. This is my own rough inference, based on unverified estimates.
- ゆっくり/ずんだもん explainers in history, geopolitics and science likely sit in the Education band (¥200–600) when they are long-form, which makes them more lucrative per view than 2ch Shorts or anime content.

### Gaps
- No source gave RPM specifically for ゆっくり解説, 2ch, 猫ミーム, or BGM/sleep genres in Japan. The figures above are mapped from broader categories.
